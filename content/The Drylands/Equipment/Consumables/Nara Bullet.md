A crude [[Nara 400er]] syringe build to fit into the barrel of a gun, very unreliable.
Spend 1 Hope to empty your magazine and load this Bullet into your Gun.
Make a Knowledge Roll against 15 - the target's Strength to administer it to a target within your range.\
On a success it heals 2 HP.
