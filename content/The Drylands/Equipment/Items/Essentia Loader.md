# ESSENTIA LOADER

Spend 1 Essentia and load it into the barrel. Make a standard weapon attack against a target within Far range. On a hit, deal d20 magic damage using your Proficiency.

_Why shoot a bullet, when you could shoot a volatile little piece of condensed magical energy?_
