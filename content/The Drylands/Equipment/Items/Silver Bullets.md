# SILVER BULLETS
Fill the magazine of your weapon with these special silver bullets. Until you reload again, all your weapon attacks deal an additional 1d6 damage, and the damage counts as physical and magical.

*Originally developed to hunt Werwolfs, works just as well when it's not a full moon.*
