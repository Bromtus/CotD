**Ridin' on the wind:** When you succees on an action roll to move up to Far Range you can spend any numer of Hope to have an equal number of willing allies within Very Close range move with you.
**Easy Ridin':** When there is no discernible hindrance, you get advantage on the action roll to move up to Far Range.
